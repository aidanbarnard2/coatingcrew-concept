Increase the durability of your factory floor

Our industrial epoxy and polyurea coatings are engineered to handle impacts and chemical spills without damage, protecting your employees and your bottom line for decades. We work with you every step of the way for a hassle-free install.

Warehouse floor coatings, ready in 24 hours

Starting with thorough floor preparation and shot-blasting, we remove weak or damaged flooring, then apply an easy-to-clean, durable coating. In as little as one day you get a smooth, even floor that is ready for full use in 24 hours.

Machine shop flooring for the toughest applications

Customizable epoxy and polyurea floors resist impacts, abrasions and chemical spills, giving you a safe, clean, low-maintenance shop.

Food production facility floors

With heavy foot traffic and machinery, safety comes first. Our innovative anti-slip coating protects employees, and our fast installation keeps downtime to a minimum.
