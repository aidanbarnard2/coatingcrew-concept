Questions we hear a lot

How long does a floor coating take?

In as little as one day, we can get your epoxy or polyurea floor coating installed and ready for full use. Most surfaces are ready for use within 24 hours.

Can you install in the winter?

Yes. We install year-round.

Can my basement floor be customized?

All of our basement floor coatings can be customized to your specifications, and we even provide one-day service to minimize the downtime of your basement space.

What warranty comes with my floor?

Many of our products come with a lifetime guarantee and a 15-year product warranty. Your floor coating specialist will provide warranty details for your specific flooring system.
