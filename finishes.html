Affordable solid color epoxy

Exceptional coverage in large areas, stands up to heavy foot traffic, minimizes odor and hides small chips and cracks. Popular in commercial kitchens, slip-resistant and available in any color.

  • Chemical and abrasion resistance
  • UV stability
  • Custom color matching

Durable solid color polyurea

A great choice when you need a quick installation: ready to go in as little as one day, and very easy to maintain.

  • Slip-resistant surface
  • Performance at any temperature
  • Long recoat window
  • Greater strength than standard flooring

Beautiful quartz flooring

Works as well for outdoor pool decks and patios as for medical facilities and warehouses. So easy to clean it is popular in commercial kitchens, locker rooms and vet clinics.

  • 100% solid formula
  • UV stability
  • Extreme adhesion
