Over 20 years of concrete experience

When we started The Coating Crew, we had over 20 years of experience in the concrete industry and a mission to offer Minneapolis-area home and business owners top-quality concrete floor coating systems. Since 1999, we’ve built a reputation for providing these superior products along with expert installation and exceptional customer service.

Trusted full-service floor and concrete restoration

As the area’s trusted full-service floor and concrete restoration company, The Coating Crew specializes in residential and light commercial applications. Our family-owned and operated business can handle both interior and exterior installations, and most surfaces will be ready for use within 24 hours.
