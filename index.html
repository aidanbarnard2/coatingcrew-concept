Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a floor coating take?+

In as little as one day, we can get your epoxy or polyurea floor coating installed and ready for full use. Most surfaces are ready for use within 24 hours.

Can you install in the winter?+

Yes. We install year-round.

What do I do with everything in my garage?+

Ask about storing your property in an on-site storage container while we coat your garage floor, through our partner Dart Portable Storage, Inc. A drop-off and pick-up fee applies. About on-site storage →

What warranty comes with my floor?+

Many of our products carry a lifetime guarantee and a 15-year product warranty. Your specialist will walk you through the coverage for your system. Our worry-free guarantee →

Do you coat commercial and industrial floors?+

Yes: retail stores, showrooms, commercial kitchens, vet clinics and kennels, locker rooms, restrooms, salons and medical centers, plus warehouses, machine shops, factories and food production facilities. Commercial · Industrial
