Retail stores

Retail floors have to look great, withstand heavy use, clean up easily and stay non-slip. Choose the custom colors of our chip system or the strength of polyurea, and be open for business in as little as a day or two.

Showrooms

In a showroom, looks matter as much as function. Our polyurea, chip, metallic, epoxy and polyurea systems all suit a showroom, and our experts will help you choose.

Kitchens and food prep

Easy cleaning and non-slip texture come first. Our coatings add a long recoat window, performance at any temperature, custom colors, pre-tinting for even color and a one-day install.

Veterinary clinics and kennels

Messes, sharp claws and spilled chemicals are no match. Our floors are easy to clean and sanitize, with slip-resistant options for people and pets.

Locker rooms

In schools, gyms and more, locker room floors must be durable, easy to clean and slip-resistant. Match your school colors, install fast and stay on budget.

Restrooms, salons and medical centers

We also provide beautiful, easy-to-clean options for restrooms, salons, medical centers and more. Tell us about your space.
